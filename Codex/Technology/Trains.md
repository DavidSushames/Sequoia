[[Industrialists]] (and probably some other factions) have built trains. They are powered with a variety of different methods.
Coal and Oil aren't a thing, so they gotta use some magic bullshit.

Trap a [[Spirit]] and feed it magic energy, and it'll do stuff for ya.
Mount this onto a train, and you can make it move, in a variety of ways.

**Air** spirits make wind to blow the train, caught by massive sails. These trains can appear rather odd, and don't handle tunnels very well, but are very cheap to run.

**Fire** spirits are used to heat up a boiler to make steam, which powers the train.

**Lightning** spirits use some kind of electromagnetism to make the train go, we don't know exactly how it works, but it does.

**Metal** spirits just accelerate the train forwards. Trains are made of metal, after all.
