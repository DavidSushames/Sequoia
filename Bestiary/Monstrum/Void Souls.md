Void Souls are [[People]] who have had their souls be corrupted by, or perhaps replaced by something from the [[Void]]. Identifiable by their solid black eyes, rude demeanor freakish strength, general disregard for the sanctity of life, and the way they just tore your buddy's arm straight out of the socket. 

They retain their memories from before, but any attachments to people is gone. 
Death is no threat, and pain is a joke.
They know anything that the Void knows, which is anything that anyone who died and wasn't claimed by a [[Gods|god]] knew.
