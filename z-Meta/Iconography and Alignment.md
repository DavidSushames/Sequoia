# Freedom
Freedom is the driving force of progress.
Freedom is changing paths, finding new ways, and leaving behind what is no longer required.
Freedom is constantly spreading and hard to stop. Freedom is escape from confines of everyday life.

Freedom is Metalworking, Water, Lizards, Lichen, Spring, and the Sun.
#### Metalworking
Metalworking is creating the tools you need to survive.
Metalworking is finding beauty in function. If it looks nice but doesn't work, it's ugly.
#### Water
Water is fluid, adaptable, seeking the path of least resistance.
Water is patient. Water and time can carve through a mountain. 
#### Lizards
Lizards shed their skin when they no longer need it.
Lizards drop their tails of doing so will save them.
#### Lichen
Lichen is difficult to stamp out. 
Lichen will grow everywhere, without permission.
#### Spring
Spring is new growth, new developments
Spring is breaking free from the cold oppression of winter.
#### Sun
The Sun is a beacon of light that guides you home. 
The Sun is light and warmth, allowing life to flourish.
# Permanence
Permanence is the ancient weight of history. 
Permanence is making sure everyone has enough, and that nothing goes to waste.
Permanence is making your mark on the world, so that you are never forgotten. 

Permanence is Swearing Oaths, Fame, Eating, Undead, Healing, and Winter.
#### Swearing Oaths
Oaths are a promise, a contract.
Oaths are proof that a thing is important.
#### Fame
Fame is proof you exist. If nobody remembers you existed, did you ever exist at all? 
Fame is your reputation.
#### Eating
Eating is power. Food production makes kingdoms.
Eating means you live.
Eating is using every part of the buffalo.
#### Undead
Undeath is keeping something going, even when it should no longer function. 
Undead is using every resource you have available.
#### Healing
Healing is restoring what is lost. 
Healing is preservation over transformation.
#### Winter
Winter preserves things, preventing decay. 
Winter is a harsh time, only the strongest traditions and bloodlines survive.
# Knowledge
Knowledge is the indomitable human spirit. 
Knowledge is overcoming the insurmountable force of nature with beautiful, ingenious artifice.

Knowledge is Persistence, Fire, Ships, Murdering, Silver, and Dogs.
#### Persistence
Persistence is doing whatever it takes to survive. 
Persistence is biting, howling, clawing, and kicking until the last beat of the heart.
#### Fire
Fire is warmth and light, driving back the night. 
Fire is a tool, cooking food and smelting ore, transforming pieces of nature into something usable. 
Fire is burning away impurities and weakness.
#### Ships
Ships are exploration manifest, crossing impossible barriers and seeking new horizons. 
Ships are the lifeblood of civilization, transporting many tons of cargo vast distances.
#### Murdering
Murdering is stopping at nothing to achieve your goals.
Murdering is making plans in advance. 
#### Silver
Silver is an enabler. Steel can get you a sword, but silver can get you an army. 
Silver can kill that which should be unkillable.
#### Dogs
Dogs is taking nature and changing it to suit your needs.
Dogs are proof that the world can be conquered and brought to heel.
# Nature
Nature is the indifferent cruelty of the universe.
Nature is the tiger lurking in the dark, plucking at that part of your brain that screams at you to runfightfreeze.

Nature is Knives, Giants, Darkness, Octopus, Toads, and Cannibalism.
#### Knives
Knives are weapons. To be unarmed is to be helpless. 
Everything in nature has a way to fight, whether that is horns, claws, teeth, poison, or camoflage.
#### Giants
Giants are massive. Being big lets you survive. Giant animals, giant trees, giant armies.
#### Darkness
Darkness is being unknowable.
Darkness is safety, where the lights of civilization can't reach you.
#### Octopuses
Octopuses are alien intelligence, making decisions that may make no sense to a logical mind.
Octopuses are strange forms and malleable biology, undefined by structure.
#### Toads
Toads are lurking in the mud, unseen but constantly growing.
Toads come in many forms, from egg to tadpole to adult.
Toads are ugly and cruel, eating anything they can fit in their mouth
#### Cannibalizing
Cannibalizing is letting nothing go to waste. Recycling.
Cannibalizing is rejecting modernity and eating your friends.
