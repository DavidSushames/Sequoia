## Overview
Black Isle is an island in southern Sacrent, surrounded by a circular bay, almost like a crater. It is known as the worst and most dangerous location in all of [[Sequoia]]. Nobody who has set foot there has returned to tell the tale.

The End is Sequoia's most secure prison. It's where they keep the worst of the worst, as well as the [[Immortals]] who they can't kill or control. Nobody, save for a few higher-ups in the [[Holy Sacrent Empire (name pending)]] knows that it exists. 
There's some kind of memory wiping magic that makes you forget everything about it. You need to row out to get picked up by someone else who never sets foot on the island.
