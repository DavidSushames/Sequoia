## Overview
Ulro is a giant ring city with high walls. It is shaped like a donut, with all the farms on the inside. Farms cannot be outside, due to the monsters of the [[Chake Shadow]] being everywhere. The limited growing space puts a cap on the population, but that's ok, people are constantly being drafted to the Wall to defend against the voidlings. Most of them don't finish their term.

Some Ulrans supplement their diets with flesh from slain voidlings. This messes with their biologies, giving them anti-magic properties. Affecting them with arcane magic is incredibly difficult, and even divine magic has difficulty. This means that they can't recieve magical healing and have to heal the slow way. Antimagic Ulrans uniforms are different, so people can identify them, so as to know who not to heal and who can survive a fireball. They have taken these uniforms and turned it into their fashion, hence the eyeliner.

Female warriors are common. Everybody must fight.
