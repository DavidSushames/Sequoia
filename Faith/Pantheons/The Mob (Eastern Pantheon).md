## Overview
The gods of the Eastern pantheon are scary, enigmatic things. 
They have spooky, evocative names like Lurker On The Edge Of Dusk, It That Lives In Savagery, Great Carrion, Desolate Tree, Wind At The End, and Wolf-That-Kills-Bears.
